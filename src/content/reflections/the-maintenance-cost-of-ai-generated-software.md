Thank you to Valley101 and Yiwen Lu for having me at Alignment 2026, and to Shawn Li (OpenAI) and Duo Ding (Cresta) for a great conversation on what it really takes to make AI work in the enterprise.

My definition of a forward-deployed engineer: someone measured on the customer’s outcome, where outcome = build quality × adoption. When both are strong, the impact multiplies, and that’s what great FDEs optimise for every day.

My prediction for 2027: 2026 was the year many companies were surprised by their token bills. 2027 will be the year of the next surprise: the maintenance cost of AI-generated software.

Building has never been easier. And like any system, AI needs care to stay valuable: models change, processes evolve, new exceptions appear, and the best systems keep improving over time. Many business cases were built around the first deployment, not year two and beyond.

So the question for 2027 isn’t just “how quickly can we build this?” It’s also “who will keep it running and getting better, and what will that really cost?” The companies that win will be the ones that see the next surprise coming and plan to run and improve what they build, not just ship it.
