Last week at the Private Markets AI Summit, four things stood out. The same four I keep hearing from clients:

1. From institutional knowledge to institutional intelligence: making shared knowledge accessible and well-categorized, and capturing how it’s used, not just what it is, to generate differentiated alpha.

2. Empowering citizen developers: without the sprawl - letting people build from the bottom up, in a way that scales instead of creating risk or runaway cost.

3. Bridging citizen development and enterprise deployment: the gap between a tool one team builds and something the whole firm can run safely, while keeping the IP and innovation in-house.

4. Getting started beats getting it perfect: pick a use case and start. Real experience from the ragged frontier beats the hype reels on LinkedIn.

Thanks to Marc Andrew and the Private Market Forum for a great day, and to the speakers for a sharp, practical program.

Happy to compare notes if your firm is working through any of these.
