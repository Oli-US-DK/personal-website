Another sample post. A sustainable cadence beats sporadic bursts — one thoughtful post every
few weeks is plenty.

A simple loop that works well: write on this site first, then share the idea on LinkedIn with
the link in the first comment rather than the post body (external links in the body get slightly
suppressed by the algorithm).

Delete this file, and [welcome-to-the-site.md](./welcome-to-the-site.md), whenever you're ready
to publish your own first post.
