Starting today as VP of Enterprise at Invisible Technologies.

Over the past few years at McKinsey, every strategy project, transaction, and transformation I worked on increasingly became "about AI." With the move to Invisible, I get to go beyond strategizing and help clients operationalize it.

Invisible Technologies has trained over 80% of the world's leading AI models. Now we're deploying that capability at enterprise scale.

What drew me in: a team that understands both the strategic ambition and the operational reality of making AI deliver -- led by CEO Matthew Fitzpatrick, former McKinsey Senior Partner and head of QuantumBlack Labs.

If you're thinking about how to accelerate your business with AI, I'd love to connect.

To my clients and colleagues at McKinsey -- thank you for an extraordinary chapter. You made the work meaningful and I look forward to staying close.
